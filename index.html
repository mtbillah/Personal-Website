Closing the loop in extrusion

An extruder logs its speed, moisture, temperatures and pressure every second. I align those signals in time with what leaves the die, then train models that run in both directions.

Process

  • Screw speed
  • Feed moisture
  • Barrel temperature
  • Specific mechanical energy
  • Die pressure and torque

Product

  • Expansion ratio
  • Bulk density
  • Texture and hardness
  • Fibrosity in meat analogues

Forward model. From what the machine is doing right now, estimate the product before it is measured. Inverse design. Start from the product you want and recover the process settings that will make it.

This work is under revision at Comprehensive Reviews in Food Science and Food Safety, and will be presented at the Cereals & Grains Association 2026 conference in Bloomington, Minnesota.

Where the work goes

Food process engineering joined with data science, aimed at extrusion and drying that are smarter, more predictable and more sustainable.

Machine learning for extrusion

Process optimization, predictive and inverse design of product quality from time-aligned process data, and model-controlled predictive systems.

Plant-based meat analogues

High-moisture extrusion, raw-material functionality, fiber and 3D printing, and computer-vision estimates of fibrosity.

High-protein, sustainable cereals and snacks

Whole corn meal, intermediate wheatgrass, and air-classified faba bean and mung bean protein concentrates.

Process engineering and modeling

Design and optimization of fluidized bed, double drum and heat pump dryers using RSM, ANN and CFD.

Sustainable food packaging

Biodegradable and starch-based films made from agricultural by-products such as banana pseudo-stem and fruit peels.
